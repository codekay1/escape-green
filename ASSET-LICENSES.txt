Escape Green — local asset licenses (v0.5.0)
================================================
All textures used by the game are generated at runtime from procedural
canvas paint routines in src/world/textures.ts (original patterns).
Nothing is fetched over the network at runtime.

HUD icons under public/icons/hud/ are original flat-vector game UI
assets. Wood and stone hotbar icons (hotbar-wood.svg, hotbar-stone.svg)
are original game UI assets in the same style.

No paid / commercial third-party packs are bundled.
Treat procedural textures as CC0-equivalent original work for this project.

## Runtime sky puff (no new file)
`texSkyPuff` in src/world/textures.ts paints a soft cloud on a canvas at startup.
It is original work in this repository, not fetched from a network, and is
CC0-equivalent like the other procedural textures. The sky gradient, sun disc,
water ripple, and foliage sway are shader code, not downloaded assets.
